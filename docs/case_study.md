# TV Spot Adaptation System - Executive Overview

## What It Does

This system transforms a single TV commercial script into **culturally-adapted versions for multiple markets** and automatically generates **visual storyboards** to preview each adaptation—all in under an hour.

## The Business Opportunity

**The Problem:**
- Global advertising campaigns need market-specific versions of TV spots (different languages, cultural references, and visual styles)
- Traditional process: Hire local creative teams, manually rewrite scripts, brief artists, wait days for storyboards
- **Result:** 3-5 days per market × multiple markets = weeks of delay and high costs

**The Solution:**
Import one master spot → AI adapts for N markets → Auto-generate storyboards → Review immediately

**Impact:**
- **Time:** 15-25 days → <1 hour for 5 markets
- **Cost:** Multiple creative teams → single automated workflow
- **Quality:** Built-in cultural compliance rules prevent missteps

---

## The Workflow (3 Simple Steps)

### **Step 1: Import Your TV Spot**

Upload a JSON file with your commercial script:
- Client/brand info
- Scene-by-scene breakdown (visual descriptions + audio cues)
- Timing for each shot

**Example:** "ACME Energy Drink - Morning Boost 30s" with 8 scenes

---

### **Step 2: Create Market Adaptations (AI-Powered)**

Select target markets from dropdown (US Hispanic, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, etc.)

**What happens:**
- AI reads your original script + market-specific cultural rules
- Adapts dialogue to natural local language (not just translation)
- Adjusts cultural references, props, locations
- Ensures compliance with local regulations
- Maintains the creative intent while respecting cultural sensitivities

**Example adaptation:**
- **Original:** "Grab your morning coffee"
- **US Hispanic:** "Prepara tu café de la mañana" (uses natural idiom, not literal translation)
- **Japanese:** References tea culture instead of coffee

⏱️ **Time:** 5 minutes per market

---

### **Step 3: Generate Storyboards (AI Image Generation)**

Click "Generate Storyboard" on any adapted version

**What happens:**
- System extracts visual description from each scene
- Adds style guidelines for consistency ("photorealistic, cinematic lighting")
- Creates images using state-of-the-art AI models (Flux, SDXL)
- Renders 3-column grid: scene thumbnails + adapted script text

⏱️ **Time:** 30 minutes for complete storyboard (runs in parallel)

## Real-World Example

**Scenario:** Launch energy drink campaign in 5 markets (US, US Hispanic, Brazil, Japan, South Korea)

**Traditional approach:**

- Write US master script: 1 week
- Hire local agencies for 4 markets: 2-3 weeks each
- Review and revise: 1-2 weeks per market
- **Total:** 10-15 weeks, $50K+ in agency fees

**With this system:**

- Write US master script: 1 week
- Import and adapt to 4 markets: 20 minutes
- Generate storyboards for all 5 versions: 2 hours
- **Total:** 1 week + 1 day, minimal additional cost
- **Savings:** 9-14 weeks, ~$40K+
